Get news alerts in Slack

We check the news every hour. Subscribe a Slack channel, and it will receive one message for each new event.

How to get a webhook URL

  1. In Slack, create an app for your workspace and turn on "Incoming Webhooks".
  2. Choose "Add New Webhook to Workspace" and pick the channel for the alerts.
  3. Copy the webhook URL and paste it below.

Details are in the Slack documentation on incoming webhooks (external site). We send a welcome message to the channel to check the webhook before we subscribe it.
